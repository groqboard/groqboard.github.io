Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the GroqBoard app (hereby referred to as "Application") for Android mobile devices and Wear OS, created by Alex Goldberg (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software, and it never asks you to create an account with the Service Provider.

What happens after that depends on which plan you use, and the difference is real, so it is set out plainly rather than glossed over:

  • Free plan, your own API key. The Service Provider operates no server in your path and collects nothing from the Application: no identifiers, no usage statistics, and no copy of your audio, transcriptions or text.
  • API key included subscription. Your requests pass through a proxy operated by the Service Provider on Cloudflare. That proxy exists for one reason: to hold the AI key so it never ships inside the app, where anyone could extract it. Your audio and text stream straight through it and are never written down. What it does keep is a usage counter and a short-lived access pass, both described under Third-Party AI Services below, and neither contains any of your content.

Everything the Application stores stays on your device: your API keys, your settings and custom vocabulary, your learned typing data, and your recent transcription history.

The Application does, however, send data to the third-party AI providers you choose, because that is how transcription and AI editing work. That flow is described in detail under Third-Party AI Services below, and it is the most important section of this policy.

This website (groqboard.com) is separate from the Application and uses Google Analytics to measure visits.

Microphone Permission

The Application requires access to your device's microphone to provide its core functionality as a voice-powered keyboard. Here's how we use this permission:

  • Voice Recording: When you tap the microphone button on the keyboard or the floating bubble, the Application temporarily records your voice to convert speech to text.
  • Processing: Audio recordings are immediately sent to the third-party speech-to-text provider you selected in Settings (Groq, OpenRouter or Soniox) for transcription. The audio is processed in real-time and converted to text. See Third-Party AI Services below for the full list.
  • No Storage: The Application does NOT store any audio recordings on your device beyond the temporary file needed for processing. Once transcription is complete, the temporary audio file is deleted.
  • No Collection: Your audio recordings are never saved by the Service Provider. On the free plan they never touch a server of the Service Provider at all: processing goes through the provider you chose, with your own API key. On the API key included subscription they pass through the Service Provider's proxy on the way to the AI provider, streamed through without being stored. Either way, no recording of yours is kept.
  • User Control: Microphone access only occurs when you actively tap the microphone button or the floating bubble. The Application never records audio in the background or without your explicit action.

Overlay Permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

The Application uses the overlay permission to display a floating voice dictation bubble over other apps. This allows you to dictate text in any app without switching keyboards.

  • Purpose: The floating bubble provides quick access to voice dictation from any screen.
  • No Data Collection: The overlay only displays the microphone bubble. It does not capture, read, or transmit any content from other apps.
  • User Control: The floating bubble is optional and can be enabled or disabled in the Application settings at any time.

Accessibility Service

The Application offers an optional Accessibility Service to enhance the floating bubble functionality. This permission is NOT required for the core keyboard to work. Here's how it is used:

  • Smart Bubble: Detects when a text input field is focused so the floating bubble can automatically appear and hide, reducing visual clutter.
  • Direct Text Insertion: Inserts transcribed text directly into the focused text field without using the clipboard, providing a seamless dictation experience.
  • Voice Editing: Reads the current text in a focused field so the AI can apply voice-commanded edits (e.g., "fix the grammar", "translate to Spanish").
  • No Screen Reading: The Accessibility Service does NOT read, capture, or transmit the contents of your screen, notifications, or any app other than the currently focused text field.
  • No Data Collection: No information obtained through the Accessibility Service is collected, stored, or transmitted to the Service Provider's servers.
  • User Control: The Accessibility Service is entirely optional. You can enable or disable it at any time in your device's Accessibility settings.

AI Text Editing (LLM)

The Application offers an optional AI text editing feature (voice editing via the "wand" button or bubble long-press). When used:

  • Processing: The current text in your focused field and your voice instruction are sent to a third-party large language model (LLM) provider: on the free plan, either Groq or OpenRouter depending on your Settings; on the API key included subscription, Groq by way of the Service Provider's proxy.
  • Custom Instructions: If you have saved personal writing-style rules or custom vocabulary in Settings, those are included in the request so the model can apply them.
  • No Storage: Neither the text nor the instruction are stored by the Application or the Service Provider. Processing happens in real time, with your own API key on the free plan and with the Service Provider's key on the subscription.
  • User Control: AI editing only activates when you explicitly long-press the wand button or bubble. It never runs automatically.

Text-to-Speech (Read Aloud)

The Application offers an optional text-to-speech feature that reads text out loud:

  • Processing: The text you ask to be read is sent to a third-party text-to-speech provider, which returns generated audio: on the free plan the one you selected in Settings (Groq, OpenRouter or Soniox), and on the API key included subscription, Groq by way of the Service Provider's proxy.
  • No Storage: The generated audio is played and discarded. Neither the text nor the audio is stored by the Application or the Service Provider.
  • User Control: Text-to-speech only runs when you explicitly trigger it.

Predictions, Autocorrect and Local Data

The Application includes a word prediction engine that learns from your typing patterns:

  • Local Storage Only: All learned prediction data (words, phrases, and typing patterns) is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. It is never transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party.
  • On-Device Typing: Autocorrect, swipe typing and the built-in dictionaries run entirely on your device with no network access.
  • AI Suggestions: When AI predictions are enabled, the text immediately surrounding your cursor (up to roughly the last 50 words of the field you are typing in) is sent to Groq so the model can suggest the next words, directly on the free plan and through the Service Provider's proxy on the subscription. This happens as you type, not only when you press a button. It is the one feature that transmits text automatically, and you can turn it off with the predictions toggle in Settings; the on-device engine above keeps working without it.
  • Text Import: You may optionally import text files (such as WhatsApp chat exports) to train the prediction engine. Imported text is processed and stored locally on your device only, and is never uploaded.
  • User Control: You can clear all prediction data by clearing the Application's data or uninstalling the app.

Transcription History

The Application keeps a short list of your most recent transcriptions so you can copy or reuse them. This history is stored only on your device, is capped at the 5 most recent entries, and is never transmitted to the Service Provider or any third party. You can clear it from Settings, or by clearing the Application's data.

Wear OS Companion

The Application includes an optional Wear OS companion for smartwatches:

  • Data Sync: Your API key, your language preferences and your custom vocabulary are synced from your phone to your watch via the Android Wearable Data Layer (a direct, encrypted connection between your paired devices). If you are on the API key included subscription, your Google Play purchase token is synced too, so the watch can obtain its own access pass and dictate on its own.
  • No Cloud Transmission: Settings are synced directly between your paired devices. They are never routed through the Service Provider's servers or any cloud service.

Third-Party AI Services

GroqBoard is a front end for third-party AI services. Every AI feature works by sending your data to an AI provider for processing. How it gets there depends on your plan.

Free plan, your own API key. Your data goes directly from your device to the provider you choose, authenticated with an API key that you create and enter yourself. The Service Provider never sees, receives, proxies or stores that data, and cannot access your provider account.

API key included subscription. Your data goes from your device to api.groqboard.com, a proxy operated by the Service Provider on Cloudflare, and from there to Groq. Your audio and text are streamed straight through it. They are not stored, not logged, and not used to train anything. Three things are kept, and only these three:

  • A 24 hour access pass: a random value stored against a one-way hash of your Google Play purchase token. The purchase token itself is never stored.
  • A usage counter tied to that same hash: seconds of audio for the day and for the month, and how many text-editing and read-aloud requests you made today. It holds no content of any kind and is deleted automatically after 40 days without use.
  • Ordinary operational logs from Cloudflare: which endpoint was called, the response status, and how long it took. These contain no audio, no text and no transcription.

The hash cannot be turned back into your identity, and the Service Provider holds no name, email or account for you. If you would rather no server of ours sit in the path at all, use the free plan with your own key, which is always available in Settings.

Depending on which features you use and which providers you select in Settings, the following data may leave your device:

  • Groq (privacy policy): receives your voice recordings for transcription, the text you ask the AI to edit along with your instruction, the text you ask to be read aloud, and, when AI predictions are enabled, the text around your cursor as you type.
  • OpenRouter (privacy policy): optional. If selected, receives your voice recordings for transcription, the text you ask the AI to edit along with your instruction, and the text you ask to be read aloud. OpenRouter routes requests to the model provider you pick, which has its own privacy terms.
  • Soniox (privacy policy): optional. If selected, receives your voice recordings for real-time transcription and the text you ask to be read aloud.

Each provider processes this data under its own privacy policy and terms of service, which govern retention and whether data may be used for model training. You are entering into that relationship directly with them through your own account, so please review the policies linked above before enabling a provider. Providers you have not configured with an API key receive nothing.

The Application does not send your data to any AI service other than the ones listed above, and no data is sent to any of them unless you have entered an API key for that provider and used a feature that requires it.

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device. The Application does not use your data for advertising, and does not sell or share your personal information with data brokers.

On the free plan the Application sends nothing to the Service Provider, so there is normally nothing to disclose. On the subscription the Service Provider holds only the access pass and the usage counter described above, neither of which contains any of your content. In the limited case of information you send directly by email (for example a support request), the Service Provider may disclose it:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • When they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

In-App Purchases

GroqBoard offers an optional monthly subscription, "API key included", processed by Google Play Billing. A one-time license sold before version 11 also remains valid for the people who bought it.

The Service Provider never receives or stores your payment information; Google Play handles it. To confirm that a subscription is active, the Application sends the Google Play purchase token to the proxy, which checks it against Google's Play Developer API and then keeps only a one-way hash of it, never the token itself.

Purchases are subject to Google Play's terms of service and to the GroqBoard Terms of Service.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network. You can also revoke individual permissions (microphone, overlay, accessibility) at any time through your device's settings.

Data Retention Policy

On the free plan the Service Provider holds no data from the Application, so there is nothing on their side to retain or delete. On the API key included subscription the Service Provider holds two things and nothing else: the access pass, which expires within 24 hours, and the usage counter, which is deleted automatically after 40 days without use. Neither contains your audio, your text or your identity. Your API keys, settings, custom vocabulary, learned prediction data and transcription history live only on your device, and you delete them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Data you have already sent to a third-party AI provider is retained according to that provider's policy and can be managed through your account with them. If you want the usage counter deleted before it expires, or you have emailed the Service Provider and want that correspondence deleted, write to support@groqboard.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.
